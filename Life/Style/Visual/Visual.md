## Design Resources

- [Background Supply](https://www.background.supply/) is a high-quality background image library for designers and creative professionals. It offers collections that vary in style, color, and theme, for web design, apps, presentations, and other creative work.
- [Cosmos](https://www.cosmos.so/home) is a visual inspiration platform for searching, collecting, and organizing images and visual references. AI search includes search by color and by visual similarity, automatic artist and source attribution, and detection and filtering of AI-generated content. It is aimed at creative professionals and teams who need an inspiration library they can search, organize, and share.
- [Hue Tools](https://hue.tools/?format=hex) is a free online color tool for designers and developers: a color picker, conversion between HEX, RGB, HSL, and CMYK, palette creation, and accessibility contrast checks.
