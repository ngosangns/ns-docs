# South Korea (Seoul)

Daytime sightseeing list:

- National Folk Museum of Korea (Bảo tàng dân gian quốc gia)
- Gwanghwamun Square
- Cheonggyecheon stream
- Starfield COEX library

## Prep

- ~50,000 KRW cash on hand
- ~25,000 KRW loaded on a T-money card
- Install a bike-rental app

## Food

- Jajangmyeon (black bean noodles)
- Skewers (꼬치)
