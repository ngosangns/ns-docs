# Vũng Tàu

Getting there: via Cần Giờ -> Vũng Tàu ferry.

## What to Pack

- Clothing: maxi dress, light jacket, hat, shoes, sandals, sunglasses, tees, long pants, shorts, tank tops,...
- Cosmetics: sunscreen, lipstick, makeup,...
- Other: cash, bank cards, ID,...

## Hotels

- Look for rooms on Trần Quý Cáp, La Văn Cầu, or Phan Văn Thị streets.

## Food

- Lẩu cá đuối (stingray hotpot) or casual ốc: quán 40 near the Nguyễn Trường Tộ gas station, opposite LOX bar.
- Bánh khọt miền Đông on Bà Triệu street.
- Bến Đình junction at night has plenty: cháo lòng, bún riêu, cơm tấm, bún thịt nướng, xôi, bánh mì... For cơm tấm and bún thịt nướng look for chú Cảnh's pushcart - 35k-40k, generous meat; when crowded you grill your own (pick a good cut, char it at the cart, hand it back to him).
- Quán bánh bèo Tuyết Mai.
- Seafood markets mostly stopped gouging now, but for the cheapest go to the market at Chí Linh junction or the seafood depot at ngã 3 Chí Linh (on the way back, ~7 km from the beach). For convenience in the center: chợ Xóm Lưới (where tourists shop), chợ ngã 3 phi trường, chợ Bến Đình.
- Buy seafood at the market, have it cooked for ~50k, then eat it on the beach.
- Late night: cháo hàu street stall at ngã ba số 9 Thùy Vân, in front of the supermarket, 50k/bowl (near the slope up to Chúa Giang Tay hill).
- Bánh khọt tôm mực quán 14, corner Hoàng Hoa Thám - Nguyễn Trường Tộ.
- Breakfast: Phở Sơn Hà 3.
- Coffee with a view: Mi Amor Beach and the shops around it.

## Ốc (Snail/Shellfish Spots)

- Ốc tự nhiên on Trần Phú.
- Gành Hào has two locations - the Trần Phú one is on the Bãi Dâu stretch.
- Budget option: Thành Phát.

## Things to Do

- Tượng Chúa Kitô dang tay (Christ statue), Hồ Mây, hồ đá xanh, the lighthouse, Thích Ca Phật Đài, đồi Con Heo.
- Evening stroll along the beach; seafood depots on Trần Phú.
- Cable car.
- **Hồ Tràm, Phước Hải:** from Vũng Tàu hug the coast toward Long Hải - you can stop at dinh Cô and mộ Cô, then take the Nước Ngọt pass out to Hồ Tràm and Hồ Cốc.
- **Hồ Tràm - Hồ Cốc - La Gi:** another coastal ride; visit dinh Thầy Thím and the La Gi lighthouse; in season the dragon-fruit gardens glow with night lights.
- **La Gi - Phan Thiết - Mũi Né:** tiny Phan Thiết city for sightseeing, then resorts in Mũi Né or out to Hòn Rơm.
