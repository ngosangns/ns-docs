# Irregular Verbs

Irregular verbs grouped by their inflection pattern: **AAA** (all three forms identical), **ABA** (V3 returns to V1), **ABB** (V2 and V3 identical), **ABC** (all three forms differ). Forms joined by `/` are both accepted variants.
