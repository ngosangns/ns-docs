# Mixed Conditionals

## Concept

- A mixed conditional combines two different conditional types in one sentence, used when the if-clause and the main clause refer to different time frames.
- Source: [Mixed conditionals – British Council LearnEnglish](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2/conditionals-third-mixed).

## Types

- **Past condition → present result (type-3 if-clause + type-2 main clause):**
  - Describes a hypothetical **past** situation with a **present** result.
  - Formula: If + S + had + V3/ed, S + would/could/might + V
  - If I had studied harder, I would have a better job now. (Nếu tôi đã học chăm chỉ hơn, bây giờ tôi đã có một công việc tốt hơn.)
  - If I hadn't got the job in Tokyo, I wouldn't be with my current partner.
- **Present condition → past result (type-2 if-clause + type-3 main clause):**
  - Describes a general **present** truth (unreal or contrary to fact) that explains a **past** result. The cause-effect direction is present → past, not past → present.
  - Formula: If + S + V2/ed, S + would/could/might + have + V3/ed
  - If I weren't so busy, I would have come to your party last night. (Nếu tôi không bận như vậy, tôi đã đến bữa tiệc của bạn tối qua rồi.)
  - It's really important. If it wasn't, I wouldn't have called you on your holiday.

## Inversion

- Mixed conditionals can be inverted like ordinary type-2 and type-3 conditionals — see [Inversion](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Inversion).
- **Original:** If he had taken better care of himself, he wouldn't be sick now.
- **Inverted:** Had he taken better care of himself, he wouldn't be sick now. (Nếu anh ấy đã chăm sóc bản thân cẩn thận hơn, anh ấy không bị ốm như bây giờ.)

## Notes

- Mixed conditionals are less common than the three basic conditional types.
- Using them correctly requires carefully identifying the time relationship between the condition and the result.

## More Examples

- If I had listened to my parents, I wouldn't be in trouble now. (Nếu tôi đã nghe lời bố mẹ, tôi đã không gặp rắc rối như bây giờ.)
- If she had taken the job, she would have been promoted by now. (Nếu cô ấy đã nhận công việc đó, bây giờ cô ấy đã được thăng chức rồi.)
