# Các mô hình biến đổi câu

## MODEL 1: Because / Since / As ↔ Because of / As a result of / Due to

**Cấu trúc:**

- **Because / Since / As** (bởi vì) + S + V ...
- **Because of / As a result of / Due to** (bởi vì) + Noun / Noun phrase / V-ing

**Ví dụ:**

- Because she behaves well, everybody loves her. → Because of her good behaviour, everybody loves her.
- Because of his bad behavior, he must be punished. → As he behaved badly, he must be punished.

## MODEL 2: Although / Though / Even though ↔ Despite / In spite of

**Cấu trúc:**

- **Although / Though / Even though** (mặc dù) + S + V ...
- **Despite / In spite of** (mặc dù) + Noun / Noun phrase / V-ing

**Ví dụ:**

- Although it rains, we go to school on time. → Despite / In spite of the rain, we go to school on time.
- Despite the noise, we continued to study our lessons. → Though it was very noisy, we continued to study our lessons.

## MODEL 3: SO ... THAT ↔ SUCH ... THAT

**Cấu trúc:**

- **SO + adjective + THAT**
- **SUCH + a/an + adjective + noun + THAT**

**Ví dụ:**

- The film was so good that I saw it three times. → It was such a good film that I saw it three times.
- It is such a bad book that nobody reads it. → This book is so bad that nobody reads it.

## MODEL 4: TOO ... + to-infinitive ↔ ENOUGH ... + to-infinitive

**Cấu trúc:**

- **TOO + adjective + to-infinitive**
- **ENOUGH + adjective + to-infinitive** (thường dùng với "not")

**Ví dụ:**

- Mary is too young to get married. → Mary isn't old enough to get married.
- The tea was not cool enough for her to drink. → The tea was too hot for her to drink.

## MODEL 5: SO ... THAT / SUCH ... THAT ↔ TOO ... + to-infinitive / ENOUGH ... + to-infinitive

**Cấu trúc:**

- **SO ... THAT / SUCH ... THAT**
- **TOO ... + to-infinitive / ENOUGH ... + to-infinitive**

**Ví dụ:**

- This raincoat is so small that she can't wear it. → This raincoat is too small for her to wear.
- It was such an interesting novel that I read it many times. → The novel was interesting enough for me to read many times.
- This question is easy enough for us to answer. → This question is so easy that we can answer.
- The box was too heavy for the child to carry. → It was such a heavy box that the child couldn't carry it.

## MODEL 6: To-infinitive ... + be + Adj./N ↔ S + V + it + Adj./N + to-infinitive

**Cấu trúc:**

- **To-infinitive ... + be + Adj./N**
- **S + V + it + Adj./N + to-infinitive**

**Ví dụ:**

- To live on my salary must be hard. → I find it hard to live on my salary.

## MODEL 7: Câu điều kiện (Conditional sentences)

**Cấu trúc:**

- **Câu ở hiện tại** → **IF + Loại 2 (Quá khứ đơn)**
- **Câu ở quá khứ** → **IF + Loại 3 (Quá khứ hoàn thành)**

**Lưu ý:**

- Clause₁ + so + clause₂ → If + clause₁..., clause₂
- Clause₁ + because + clause₂ → If + clause₂..., clause₁

**Ví dụ:**

- He can't do the test because it is too difficult. → If the test weren't too difficult, he could do it.
- I didn't eat anything so I was hungry. → I wouldn't have been hungry if I had eaten something.

## MODEL 8: If...not ↔ Unless

**Cấu trúc:**

- **If...not**
- **Unless...**

**Lưu ý:** Không được thay đổi loại điều kiện, chỉ thay đổi loại điều kiện đã cho.

**Ví dụ:**

- Unless it rains, we will go to the movies (loại 1) → If it doesn't rain, we will go to the movies. (loại 1)
- If they didn't attend class regularly, they couldn't understand the lesson (loại 2) → Unless they attended class regularly, they couldn't understand the lesson. (loại 2)

## MODEL 9: S + V(present perfect) .... + time ↔ It is + time ... + since ... + V(past)

**Cấu trúc:**

- **S + V(present perfect) .... + time**
- **It is + time ... + since ... + V(past)**

**Ví dụ:**

- Tom and Mary have been married for five years. → It is five years since Tom and Mary were married.
- I haven't seen my father for one month. → It's one month since I last saw my father.

## MODEL 10: Câu ao ước (Wish sentences)

**Cấu trúc:**

- **Câu ở tương lai** → **Wish + would + V₁**
- **Câu ở hiện tại** → **Wish + Quá khứ đơn**
- **Câu ở quá khứ** → **Wish + Quá khứ hoàn thành**

**Ví dụ:**

- She won't visit me again. → I wish she would visit me again.
- My wife can't speak French. → I wish my wife could speak French.
- What a pity you failed in your driving test. → I wish you hadn't failed in your driving test.

## MODEL 11: S + have/has + not + V3/ed ... since (for)... ↔ S + last + V(past) ... when... / The last time + S + was ....

**Cấu trúc:**

- **S + have/has + not + V3/ed ... since (for)...**
- **S + last + V(past) ... when...**
- **The last time + S + was ....**

**Ví dụ:**

- I haven't heard him since August. → The last time I heard him was in August.
- I haven't seen him since I was a student. → I last saw him when I was a student.

## MODEL 12: S + V(past) ..... ago ↔ S + have/has + been + V-ing + since (for)...

**Cấu trúc:**

- **S + V(past) ..... ago**
- **S + have/has + been + V-ing + since (for)...**

## MODEL 13: S + V + ..... + time ↔ It take/took + (Object) + time + to-infinitive...

**Cấu trúc:**

- **S + V + ..... + time**
- **It take/took + (Object) + time + to-infinitive...**

**Nghĩa:** (Ai mất thời gian bao lâu để làm gì)

**Ví dụ:**

- She typed the letter in 20 minutes. → It took her 20 minutes to type the letter.
- I walked to my office in 10 minutes. → It took me 10 minutes to walk to my office.

## MODEL 14: S + didn't +V₁... until... ↔ It was not until ... that...

**Cấu trúc:**

- **S + didn't +V₁... until...**
- **It was not until ... that...**

**Nghĩa:** (Mãi cho đến khi...)

**Ví dụ:**

- Tom didn't begin to read until he was eight. → It was not until Tom was eight that he began to read.

## MODEL 15: Thể truyền khiến (Causative Form)

### Câu chủ động (Active Voice)

**Cấu trúc:**

- **S + have + Object₁ + V₁ + Object₂**
- **S + get + Object₁ + to-infinitive + Object₂**

**Ví dụ:**

- I will have Tom wash the car.
- I will get Tom to wash the car.

### Câu bị động (Passive Voice)

**Cấu trúc:**

- **S + have/get + Object + V₃/ed**

**Ví dụ:**

- I will have the car washed.
- I will get the car washed.

## MODEL 16: Câu bị động (Passive Voice)

**Cấu trúc:**

- **S + V + Object ...** (Câu chủ động)
- **S + be + V₃/ed ... + (by) ...** (Câu bị động)

**Ví dụ:**

- The police is questioning him. → He is being questioned by the police.

## MODEL 17: So sánh hơn ↔ So sánh nhất

**Cấu trúc:**

- **SO SÁNH HƠN**
- **SO SÁNH NHẤT**

**Ví dụ:**

- My mother was the most warm-hearted person I've ever known. → I've never known a more warm-hearted person than my mother.

## MODEL 18: Câu bị động với động từ tường thuật (Passive voice for verbs of reporting/thinking)

### Câu chủ động (Active Voice)

**Cấu trúc:**

- **People / they + V₁ (consider/ know / believe/ say /...) + ...that + S + V₂ + ...**

### Câu bị động (Passive Voice)

**Form 1 (Impersonal Passive with 'It'):**

- **It + is/ was + V₃/ed (của V₁) + that + S + V₂ +...**

**Form 2 (Personal Passive with Infinitive):**

- **S + be + V₃/ed (của V₁) + to infinitive ....** (khi V₂ ở hiện tại hoặc tương lai)
- **S + be + V₃/ed (của V₁) + to have + V₃/ed....** (khi V₂ ở quá khứ)

**Ví dụ:**

- The owner of the house is thought to be abroad.
- People thought that he had died in battle. → It was thought that he had died in battle. → He was thought to have died in battle.

## MODEL 19: So sánh bằng ↔ So sánh hơn

**Cấu trúc:**

- **SO SÁNH BẰNG**
- **SO SÁNH HƠN**

**Ví dụ:**

- Susan isn't as good at chemistry as Sarah. → Sarah is better at chemistry than Susan.

## MODEL 20: So sánh kép (Double Comparative)

**Cấu trúc:**

- **Clause₁ + Clause₂**
- **SO SÁNH KÉP**

**Ví dụ:**

- As he grows older, he becomes increasingly cheerful. → The older he grows, the more cheerful he becomes.

## MODEL 21: It take sb+khoảng thời gian+to do sth ↔ sb spend +khoảng thời gian doing sth

**Cấu trúc:**

- **It take sb+khoảng thời gian+to do sth**
- **sb spend +khoảng thời gian doing sth**

**Ví dụ:**

- It took her 3 hours to do her homework → She spent 3 hours doing her homework.

## MODEL 22: Understand ↔ to be aware of

**Cấu trúc:**

- **Understand**
- **to be aware of**

**Ví dụ:**

- Do you understand the lesson? → Are you aware of the lesson?

## MODEL 23: To be interested in = fond of = keen on = like

**Cấu trúc:**

- **To be interested in = fond of = keen on = like**

**Ví dụ:**

- She likes English → She is interested in English

## MODEL 24: Succeed in doing sth ↔ manage to do sth

**Cấu trúc:**

- **Succeed in doing sth**
- **manage to do sth**

**Ví dụ:**

- We succeeded in inventing television → We managed to invent television

## MODEL 25: Prefer sb to do sth ↔ Would rather sb +did sth (muốn)

**Cấu trúc:**

- **Prefer sb to do sth**
- **Would rather sb +did sth** (muốn)

**Ví dụ:**

- I prefer you not to smoke here → I'd rather you not smoked here

## MODEL 26: Prefer doing sth to doing sth ↔ would rather do sth than do sth / like doing sth better than doing sth

**Cấu trúc:**

- **Prefer doing sth to doing sth**
- **would rather do sth than do sth / like doing sth better than doing sth** (thích làm)

**Ví dụ:**

- She prefers staying at home to going out → She would rather stay at home than go out

## MODEL 27: As soon as+S+V2/ed,S+V2/ed ↔ Hardly +had+S+V3/ed+when+S+V2/ed ↔ No sooner+had+S+V3/ed+than+S+V2/ed

**Cấu trúc:**

- **As soon as+S+V2/ed,S+V2/ed**
- **Hardly +had+S+V3/ed+when+S+V2/ed**
- **No sooner+had+S+V3/ed+than+S+V2/ed**

**Lưu ý:** Ta thấy mệnh đề đứng sau sau as soon as sẽ là mệnh đề sau when và sau than

**Ví dụ:**

- As soon as I left the house, he appeared → Hardly had I left the house when he appeared

## MODEL 28: Not...............any more ↔ No longer+auxiliary+S+V / No more.........

**Cấu trúc:**

- **Not...............any more**
- **No longer+auxiliary+S+V / No more.........**

## MODEL 29: To be not worth doing sth ↔ There is no point in doing sth

**Cấu trúc:**

- **To be not worth doing sth**
- **There is no point in doing sth**

**Nghĩa:** không đáng, vô ích

**Ví dụ:**

- It is not worth making him get up early → There is no point in making him get up early

## MODEL 30: It seems that+S+V ↔ It is likely that+S+V ↔ It look as if/though

**Cấu trúc:**

- **It seems that+S+V**
- **It is likely that+S+V**
- **It look as if/though**

**Nghĩa:** dường như rằng

**Ví dụ:**

- It seems that he will come late → It is likely that he will come late = He is likely to come late / It looks as if he will come late

## MODEL 31: S+often +V ↔ S+be+accustomed to doing=S+be/get +used to doing

**Cấu trúc:**

- **S+often +V**
- **S+be+accustomed to doing=S+be/get +used to doing**

**Ví dụ:**

- Lan often cries when she meets with difficulties → Lan is used to crying when she meets with difficulties

## MODEL 32: This is the first time+S+have/has+V3/ed ↔ S+be+not used to doing

**Cấu trúc:**

- **This is the first time+S+have/has+V3/ed**
- **S+be+not used to doing**

**Ví dụ:**

- This is the first time I have seen so many people crying at the end of the movie → I was not used to seeing so many people crying at the end of the movie

## MODEL 33: S+V+because+S+V ↔ S+V+to V

**Cấu trúc:**

- **S+V+because+S+V**
- **S+V+to V**

**Ví dụ:**

- She studies hard because she wants to pass the final examination → She studies hard to pass the final examination

## MODEL 34: S+V+not only... +but also ↔ S+V+both....and.....

**Cấu trúc:**

- **S+V+not only... +but also**
- **S+V+both....and.....**

**Ví dụ:**

- He translated fast and correctly → He translated not only fast but also correctly

## MODEL 35: S+ask/want +O+to V ↔ S+get+O+to do sth=S+have+O+do sth

**Cấu trúc:**

- **S+ask/want +O+to V**
- **S+get+O+to do sth=S+have+O+do sth**

**Ví dụ:**

- I ask him to spend more time with me → I have him spend more time with me

## MODEL 36: S+V+no+N ↔ S+be+N-less

**Cấu trúc:**

- **S+V+no+N**
- **S+be+N-less**

**Ví dụ:**

- She always speaks no care = She is always careless about her words

## MODEL 37: S+be+adj+prep ↔ S+V+adv

**Cấu trúc:**

- **S+be+adj+prep**
- **S+V+adv**

**Ví dụ:**

- My students are very good at maths → My students study maths well

## MODEL 38: It+be+adj ↔ What +a/an+adj+N

**Cấu trúc:**

- **It+be+adj**
- **What +a/an+adj+N**

**Ví dụ:**

- It was an interesting film → what an interesting film

## MODEL 39: S+V+adv ↔ How+adj+S+be...

**Cấu trúc:**

- **S+V+adv**
- **How+adj+S+be...**

**Ví dụ:**

- She washes clothes quickly → How quick she is to wash clothes

## MODEL 40: If I were you, I would+Vo ↔ S+advise+sb+to do

**Cấu trúc:**

- **If I were you, I would+Vo**
- **S+advise+sb+to do**

**Ví dụ:**

- "If I were you, I wouldn't do that" He said → He advised me not to do that
