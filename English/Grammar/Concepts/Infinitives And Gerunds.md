# Infinitives And Gerunds

## Infinitive

- **Form:** to + base verb (to eat, to sleep, to go).
- **Functions:**
  - Subject: To learn English is important. (Học tiếng Anh là quan trọng.)
  - Object of a verb: I want to travel the world. (Tôi muốn đi du lịch vòng quanh thế giới.)
  - Complement of a noun: His dream is to become a doctor. (Ước mơ của anh ấy là trở thành bác sĩ.)
  - Complement of an adjective: It is difficult to understand him. (Thật khó hiểu anh ta.)
  - Adverbial of purpose: She went to the store to buy some milk. (Cô ấy đến cửa hàng để mua sữa.)

## Gerund

- **Form:** verb + -ing (eating, sleeping, going).
- **Functions:**
  - Subject: Swimming is good exercise. (Bơi lội là một bài tập tốt.)
  - Object of a verb: I enjoy reading books. (Tôi thích đọc sách.)
  - Object of a preposition: He is good at playing the piano. (Anh ấy giỏi chơi piano.)

## Infinitive vs Gerund

- **After certain verbs:**
  - Verbs followed only by an infinitive: want, decide, hope, agree, refuse, promise, plan, learn, expect, afford.
  - Verbs followed only by a gerund: enjoy, mind, finish, avoid, consider, suggest, deny, admit, miss, risk.
  - Verbs that take both — with a change of meaning: remember, forget, regret, stop, try.
- **After certain phrases:**
  - Phrases followed only by an infinitive: be able to, be about to, be going to, in order to, so as to.
  - Phrases followed only by a gerund: be used to, be accustomed to, be good at, be bad at, be interested in, look forward to.

## Other Notes

- To negate an infinitive, put "not" before "to": I decided not to go to the party.
- To negate a gerund, put "not" before it: He denied not stealing the money.
- A gerund can take the perfect form (having + V3/ed) for an action that happened before the main action: He regretted having said that.

Choosing correctly between the infinitive and the gerund is one of the core points of English grammar — regular practice is the only reliable way to internalize the verb lists.
