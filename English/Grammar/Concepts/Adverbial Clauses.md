# Adverbial Clauses

## Concept

- An adverbial clause is a dependent clause that functions like an adverb, modifying a verb, an adjective, another adverb, or the whole main clause.
- It normally starts with a subordinating conjunction and contains its own subject and verb.

## Types of Adverbial Clauses

- **Time:** when, while, as, before, after, since, until, as soon as, whenever
  - I will call you when I get home. (Tôi sẽ gọi cho bạn khi tôi về nhà.)
- **Place:** where, wherever, everywhere
  - I will go wherever you go. (Tôi sẽ đi bất cứ nơi nào bạn đi.)
- **Reason:** because, since, as, now that
  - I am happy because I passed the exam. (Tôi vui vì tôi đã đậu kỳ thi.)
- **Purpose:** so that, in order that
  - I study hard so that I can get a good job. (Tôi học chăm chỉ để có thể có một công việc tốt.)
- **Result:** so...that, such...that
  - It was so hot that I couldn't sleep. (Trời nóng đến nỗi tôi không thể ngủ được.)
- **Condition:** if, unless, as long as, provided that — see the conditional section of [Sentence Structure](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Sentence Structure) and [Mixed Conditionals](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Mixed Conditionals).
  - If it rains, we will stay at home. (Nếu trời mưa, chúng tôi sẽ ở nhà.)
- **Concession:** although, though, even though, even if
  - Although it was raining, we went for a walk. (Mặc dù trời mưa, chúng tôi vẫn đi dạo.)
- **Manner:** as, as if, as though
  - She sings as if she were a professional singer. (Cô ấy hát như thể cô ấy là một ca sĩ chuyên nghiệp.)
- **Comparison:** as...as, than, the...the
  - He is taller than his brother. (Anh ấy cao hơn anh trai mình.)

## Position in a Sentence

- An adverbial clause can come before, inside, or after the main clause.
- When it opens the sentence, it is normally separated from the main clause by a comma: _Although it was raining, we went for a walk._
