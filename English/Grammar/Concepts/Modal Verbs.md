# Modal Verbs

Modal verbs are auxiliary verbs that add **ability, permission, obligation, advice, or likelihood** to the main verb.

**General formula:** S + modal + V (bare infinitive)

- Modals have **no -s** in the third person, **no "to"** after them (except ought to / have to / be able to / used to), and form questions/negatives without "do".
- _She can swim._ · _Can she swim?_ · _She cannot (can't) swim._

## Core Functions

### Ability

| Modal      | Meaning                       | Example                                                              |
| ---------- | ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| can        | present ability               | _I can speak English._ (Tôi có thể nói tiếng Anh.)                   |
| could      | past ability                  | _She could read when she was four._                                  |
| be able to | all tenses; one managed event | _I was able to finish on time._ (Tôi đã có thể hoàn thành đúng hạn.) |

### Permission and Requests

| Modal | Register             | Example                                  |
| ----- | -------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| can   | informal permission  | _Can I borrow your pen?_                 |
| could | polite request       | _Could you open the window?_             |
| may   | formal permission    | _May I come in?_ (Tôi có thể vào không?) |
| might | very polite          | _Might I suggest a different approach?_  |
| would | polite request/offer | _Would you like some coffee?_            |

### Obligation, Prohibition, and Necessity

| Modal                   | Meaning                                    | Example                                                     |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| must                    | strong obligation (speaker's view)         | _You must stop at a red light._ (Bạn phải dừng khi đèn đỏ.) |
| have to                 | external obligation (rules, circumstances) | _I have to wear a uniform at work._                         |
| mustn't                 | prohibition                                | _You mustn't smoke here._ (Bạn không được hút thuốc ở đây.) |
| don't have to / needn't | no necessity                               | _You don't have to come early._ (Bạn không cần đến sớm.)    |

**Warning:** _mustn't_ (forbidden) and _don't have to_ (not necessary) are opposites, not synonyms.

### Advice and Expectation

| Modal             | Meaning                      | Example                                              |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| should / ought to | advice, the right thing      | _You should see a doctor._ (Bạn nên đi khám bác sĩ.) |
| had better        | strong advice with a warning | _You'd better hurry or you'll be late._              |
| shall             | offers/suggestions (I/we)    | _Shall we go?_ (Chúng ta đi nhé?)                    |
| needn't           | no necessity                 | _You needn't worry._                                 |

## Deduction and Possibility

How certain the speaker is about the present/future:

| Degree        | Modal               | Example                                                    |
| ------------- | ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Certain (yes) | must                | _He must be tired after the trip._ (Chắc chắn anh ấy mệt.) |
| Possible      | may / might / could | _It may rain later._ (Trời có thể mưa lát nữa.)            |
| Certain (no)  | can't               | _That can't be true._ (Điều đó không thể đúng.)            |

## Perfect Modals (Modal + have + V3/ed)

For speculation or regret about the **past**:

| Structure                 | Meaning                                                                                           | Example                                                                 |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| must have + V3            | certain it happened                                                                               | _She must have left already._ (Chắc cô ấy đã rời đi rồi.)               |
| can't/couldn't have + V3  | certain it didn't happen                                                                          | _He can't have stolen it — he was abroad._                              |
| may/might have + V3       | possibly happened                                                                                 | _They might have missed the bus._ (Có thể họ đã lỡ xe buýt.)            |
| could have + V3           | was possible but didn't happen                                                                    | _You could have hurt yourself!_ (Bạn suýt tự làm mình bị thương!)       |
| should/ought to have + V3 | regret — the right action not taken                                                               | _You should have told me earlier._ (Lẽ ra bạn nên nói với tôi sớm hơn.) |
| needn't have + V3         | done, but unnecessarily                                                                           | _You needn't have cooked — we ordered pizza._                           |
| would have + V3           | unreal past result — see [Conditional Sentences](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Conditional Sentences) | _I would have helped if you had asked._                                 |

## Semi-Modals

Behave partly like modals: **have to, be able to, be supposed to, be allowed to, used to, had better, would rather, need (to), dare**.

- _You are supposed to wear a helmet._ (Bạn được yêu cầu đội mũ bảo hiểm.)
- For **used to** and **would rather**, see [Used To And Be Used To](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Used To And Be Used To) and [Subjunctive Mood](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Subjunctive Mood).

## Notes

- One modal per verb — "must to go" and "can to swim" are wrong.
- For tense variety, swap in a semi-modal: _will be able to_ (future of can), _had to_ (past of must).
- Modals in the [passive](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Passive Voice): _The rules must be followed._
