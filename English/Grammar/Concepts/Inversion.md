# Inversion

An inverted sentence puts the **auxiliary or main verb before the subject** for emphasis or a formal tone.

## Inversion with Negative Adverbs

Adverbs like **never, hardly, seldom, rarely, little, only** can open a sentence, forcing subject–auxiliary inversion.

**Formula:** Negative adverb + auxiliary + S + main verb

- _Never have I seen such a beautiful sunset._ (Tôi chưa bao giờ thấy hoàng hôn đẹp như vậy.)
- _Seldom does he go to the gym._ (Anh ấy hiếm khi đi đến phòng tập gym.)
- _Little did she know about the danger ahead._ (Cô ấy ít biết về nguy hiểm phía trước.)

## Inversion with "Only"

When a phrase starting with "only" opens the sentence, the main clause inverts.

**Formula:** Only if/when/after/by + clause + auxiliary + S + main verb

- _Only after he left did I realize my mistake._ (Chỉ sau khi anh ấy rời đi, tôi mới nhận ra lỗi lầm của mình.)
- _Only when she arrived did we start the meeting._ (Chỉ khi cô ấy đến, chúng tôi mới bắt đầu cuộc họp.)

## Inversion with "No Sooner" and "Hardly"

Describes two consecutive actions.

**Formulas:**

- No sooner + had + S + V3 + than + S + V2
- Hardly/Scarcely + had + S + V3 + when + S + V2

- _No sooner had I closed the door than he knocked._ (Tôi vừa đóng cửa thì anh ta gõ cửa.)
- _Hardly had she finished her meal when the phone rang._ (Cô ấy vừa ăn xong thì điện thoại reo.)

## Inversion with "So" and "Such"

Emphasizes degree.

**Formulas:**

- So + adj/adv + auxiliary + S + main verb
- Such + (a/an) + adj + noun + auxiliary + S + main verb

- _So beautiful was the scenery that I couldn't stop looking at it._ (Khung cảnh đẹp đến mức tôi không thể ngừng nhìn.)
- _Such a difficult exam was it that many students failed._ (Bài kiểm tra khó đến nỗi nhiều học sinh trượt.)

## Inversion in Conditionals

Dropping "if" and fronting the auxiliary produces a formal conditional:

- Type 2: _Were I you, I would accept the job._ (= If I were you...)
- Type 3: _Had he taken better care of himself, he wouldn't be sick now._ — see [Mixed Conditionals](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Mixed Conditionals).
