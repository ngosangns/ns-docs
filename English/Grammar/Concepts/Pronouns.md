# Pronouns

A pronoun replaces a noun or noun phrase to avoid repetition.

## Personal Pronouns

|         | Subject   | Object     | Possessive adjective (determiner) | Possessive pronoun | Reflexive              |
| ------- | --------- | ---------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------ | ---------------------- |
| 1st sg. | I         | me         | my                                | mine               | myself                 |
| 2nd     | you       | you        | your                              | yours              | yourself / yourselves  |
| 3rd sg. | he/she/it | him/her/it | his/her/its                       | his/hers/—         | himself/herself/itself |
| 1st pl. | we        | us         | our                               | ours               | ourselves              |
| 3rd pl. | they      | them       | their                             | theirs             | themselves             |

- **Subject pronouns** do the action: _She loves him._
- **Object pronouns** receive it, or follow prepositions: _He loves her._ · _Come with me._
- **Possessive adjectives** precede a noun: _my book._ **Possessive pronouns** stand alone: _The book is mine._

## Reflexive Pronouns

- Same subject and object: _She taught herself to code._ (Cô ấy tự học lập trình.)
- Emphasis: _I did it myself._ (Tôi tự làm điều đó.)
- **by + reflexive** = alone: _He lives by himself._ (Anh ấy sống một mình.)
- After certain verbs in fixed expressions: enjoy, help, behave, pride, blame + oneself.

## Demonstrative Pronouns

- **this/these** — near in space or time: _This is my phone._
- **that/those** — far: _That was a great movie._

## Indefinite Pronouns

|        | -one / -body       | -thing     | -where     |
| ------ | ------------------ | ---------- | ---------- |
| some-  | someone/somebody   | something  | somewhere  |
| any-   | anyone/anybody     | anything   | anywhere   |
| every- | everyone/everybody | everything | everywhere |
| no-    | no one/nobody      | nothing    | nowhere    |

- **some-** in affirmative sentences and offers: _Someone called you._ · _Would you like something to eat?_
- **any-** in negatives and questions: _Did anyone call?_ · _I don't know anything._
- **no-** already negative — never with "not": _Nobody came._ (not "didn't nobody")
- All take a **singular verb**: _Everybody is here._

## Interrogative Pronouns

**who, whom, whose, which, what** — ask about people or things; see [Questions And Question Tags](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Questions And Question Tags).

## Reciprocal Pronouns

- **each other / one another:** _They help each other._ (Họ giúp đỡ lẫn nhau.)

## One / Ones

Substitute for a countable noun already mentioned:

- _Which jacket do you like? — The red **one**._
- _I prefer the black **ones**._

## It — Special Uses

"It" is also a dummy subject for time, weather, distance, and anticipatory structures — see [There Be And It](/English/Grammar/Concepts/There Be And It).

## Notes

- Possessive pronouns never take an apostrophe: _yours, hers, its, ours, theirs_ — "it's" means "it is".
- For agreement rules with indefinite pronouns (each, either, neither, none), see [Subject Verb Agreement](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Subject Verb Agreement).
- For **relative** pronouns (who, which, that, whose), see [Relative Clauses](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Relative Clauses).
