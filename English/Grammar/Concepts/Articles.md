# Articles

English has three article choices before a noun: **a/an** (indefinite), **the** (definite), or **zero article** (no article).

## A / An — Indefinite Article

Used with **singular countable nouns** when the listener does not know exactly which one — first mention, "one of many", a member of a group.

- **a** before a consonant **sound**: a dog, a university (yoo-), a European country.
- **an** before a vowel **sound**: an apple, an hour (silent h), an MBA (em-).

Uses:

- First mention: _I saw a dog in the street._ (Tôi thấy một con chó trên phố.)
- Jobs/roles: _She is a teacher._ (Cô ấy là giáo viên.)
- Meaning "per/every": _twice a week, 60 km an hour._
- Meaning "one": _I'll be back in a minute._

## The — Definite Article

Used with any noun (singular, plural, uncountable) when **both speaker and listener know which one** — specific or unique.

- Second mention: _I saw a dog. The dog was chasing a cat._
- Unique things: _the sun, the moon, the internet, the environment._
- Superlatives and ordinals: _the best, the first, the only._
- Rivers, seas, oceans, mountain ranges, deserts: _the Mekong, the Pacific, the Alps, the Sahara._
- Groups of people: _the rich, the elderly, the unemployed._
- Musical instruments: _play the piano._
- Decades/centuries: _the 1990s, the 21st century._

## Zero Article — No Article

- **Plural and uncountable nouns in general statements:** _Dogs are loyal._ · _Water is essential._ (Chó trung thành. Nước là thiết yếu.)
- **Proper names:** countries, cities, continents, single mountains, lakes: _Vietnam, Hanoi, Asia, Mount Fansipan, Lake Baikal._
- **Meals, languages, most sports:** _have breakfast, speak English, play football._
- **Fixed phrases:** at home, at school, at work, go to bed, by bus/car/train, day and night.
- **Abstract nouns in general:** _Love is blind._ · _Time flies._

## Quick Comparison

| Context                                 | Article | Example                     |
| --------------------------------------- | ------- | --------------------------- |
| First mention, one of many              | a/an    | _I bought a book._          |
| Known to both, unique, mentioned before | the     | _The book was interesting._ |
| General plural/uncountable              | zero    | _Books are expensive._      |

## Notes

- "A" vs "an" follows **pronunciation**, not spelling: an honest man, a useful tool.
- Some nouns change meaning by article: _go to school_ (as a student) vs _go to the school_ (to the building); _in hospital_ (as a patient) vs _in the hospital_.
- Names with "the": countries that are unions or plurals — _the United States, the Philippines, the Netherlands._
