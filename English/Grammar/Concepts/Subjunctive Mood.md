# Subjunctive Mood

The subjunctive expresses **unreal situations, wishes, suggestions, or demands**.

## With Verbs of Suggestion and Demand

Verbs like **suggest, recommend, request, demand, insist, advise** take a that-clause whose verb is the bare infinitive.

**Formula:** S + V + that + S + (should) + V (bare)

- _I suggest that he **go** to the doctor._ (Tôi đề nghị anh ấy đi khám bác sĩ.)
- _She insisted that he **be** on time._ (Cô ấy khăng khăng rằng anh ấy phải đúng giờ.)

## With "It is important/necessary/essential that"

The same pattern follows **It is important/necessary/essential/vital...**

- _It is necessary that everyone **be** present at the meeting._ (Cần thiết rằng mọi người có mặt trong cuộc họp.)
- _It is important that she **submit** her assignment on time._ (Điều quan trọng là cô ấy phải nộp bài đúng hạn.)

## With "If" / "Wish" / "As if"

### Type-2 and type-3 conditionals (unreal present or past)

- _If I were you, I would accept the job._ (Nếu tôi là bạn, tôi sẽ nhận công việc đó.)
- _If he had studied harder, he would have passed the exam._ (Nếu anh ấy học chăm hơn, anh ấy đã đậu kỳ thi rồi.)

See [Sentence Structure](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Sentence Structure) for the three conditional types and [Mixed Conditionals](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Mixed Conditionals) for mixed time frames.

### "Wish" for wishes

- _I wish I **had more money**._ (Tôi ước tôi có nhiều tiền hơn.)
- _She wishes she **had studied** harder._ (Cô ấy ước mình đã học chăm chỉ hơn.)

### "As if / As though" for unreal comparisons

- _He talks as if he **knew everything**._ (Anh ấy nói như thể anh ấy biết mọi thứ.)
- _She behaves as though she **were the boss**._ (Cô ấy cư xử như thể cô ấy là sếp.)
