# Verb Tenses

English has **twelve tenses**: three time frames (present, past, future) × four aspects (simple, continuous, perfect, perfect continuous).

## Present Tenses

| Tense                      | Formula                     | Use                                             | Example                                                         |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Present simple             | S + V(s/es)                 | habits, facts, schedules, general truths        | _She works in a bank._ (Cô ấy làm việc ở ngân hàng.)            |
| Present continuous         | S + am/is/are + V-ing       | actions happening now, temporary situations     | _I am studying English now._ (Tôi đang học tiếng Anh.)          |
| Present perfect            | S + have/has + V3/ed        | past actions linked to now; experience; results | _I have finished my homework._ (Tôi đã làm xong bài tập.)       |
| Present perfect continuous | S + have/has + been + V-ing | ongoing actions that started in the past        | _She has been waiting for two hours._ (Cô ấy đã chờ hai tiếng.) |

**Signal words:**

- Present simple: always, usually, often, every day/week, on Mondays.
- Present continuous: now, at the moment, at present, Look!, Listen!.
- Present perfect: just, already, yet, ever, never, since, for, so far, recently.
- Present perfect continuous: since, for, all day, how long.

## Past Tenses

| Tense                   | Formula                | Use                                      | Example                                                               |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Past simple             | S + V2/ed              | finished actions at a definite past time | _He visited Hanoi last year._ (Anh ấy đã đến Hà Nội năm ngoái.)       |
| Past continuous         | S + was/were + V-ing   | actions in progress at a past moment     | _I was sleeping at 10 pm._ (Tôi đang ngủ lúc 10 giờ tối.)             |
| Past perfect            | S + had + V3/ed        | the earlier of two past actions          | _She had left before I arrived._ (Cô ấy đã rời đi trước khi tôi đến.) |
| Past perfect continuous | S + had + been + V-ing | ongoing action before another past event | _He had been working all day when I called._                          |

**Signal words:**

- Past simple: yesterday, ago, last week/year, in 2010.
- Past continuous: at this time yesterday, while, when.
- Past perfect: before, after, by the time, already (in past context).

**Past continuous vs past simple:** a longer ongoing action (past continuous) is often interrupted by a shorter one (past simple): _While I was cooking, the phone rang._ (Trong khi tôi đang nấu ăn, điện thoại reo.)

## Future Tenses

| Tense                     | Formula                    | Use                                      | Example                                                               |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Future simple             | S + will + V               | predictions, instant decisions, promises | _I think it will rain._ (Tôi nghĩ trời sẽ mưa.)                       |
| Future continuous         | S + will be + V-ing        | action in progress at a future moment    | _This time tomorrow I will be flying._ (Giờ này mai tôi sẽ đang bay.) |
| Future perfect            | S + will have + V3/ed      | completed before a future point          | _By 2030, I will have graduated._ (Đến 2030, tôi sẽ đã tốt nghiệp.)   |
| Future perfect continuous | S + will have been + V-ing | duration up to a future point            | _By May, she will have been teaching for ten years._                  |

**Other ways of talking about the future:**

- **be going to + V:** plans and intentions made before speaking, or predictions based on present evidence.
  - _I am going to visit my grandmother this weekend._ (Tôi dự định thăm bà cuối tuần này.)
  - _Look at the clouds — it is going to rain._ (Nhìn mây kìa — trời sắp mưa.)
- **Present continuous:** fixed arrangements with a time and place already set.
  - _We are meeting the client at 9 am tomorrow._ (Chúng tôi gặp khách hàng lúc 9 giờ sáng mai.)
- **Present simple:** timetables and schedules.
  - _The train leaves at 6:30._ (Tàu khởi hành lúc 6 giờ 30.)

## Time Clauses and the Future

In [adverbial clauses](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Adverbial Clauses) of time and condition, use a **present tense** — not "will" — for future meaning.

- _I will call you when I get home._ (Tôi sẽ gọi cho bạn khi tôi về nhà.) — not "when I will get"
- _As soon as she arrives, we will start._ (Ngay khi cô ấy đến, chúng ta sẽ bắt đầu.)
- _If it rains, we will stay home._ — see [Conditional Sentences](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Conditional Sentences).

## Stative Verbs

Stative verbs describe states, not actions, and are normally **not used in continuous tenses**.

- **Mind/thought:** know, believe, understand, mean, remember, forget, realize, suppose.
- **Emotion:** love, hate, like, want, need, prefer, wish.
- **Senses:** see, hear, smell, taste, feel (when describing perception, not deliberate action).
- **Possession/other:** have (own), own, belong, contain, cost, seem, matter.

_She owns three cars._ — not "is owning". Some verbs shift meaning in the continuous: _I think it is wrong_ (believe) vs _I am thinking of moving_ (considering); _I have a car_ (own) vs _I am having lunch_ (eating).

## Notes

- Master the **formula + use + signal words** triplet for each tense — exam questions usually hinge on one of the three.
- Present perfect vs past simple is the most common confusion: a definite past time (yesterday, in 2010) forces the past simple.
- For past habits, see [Used To And Be Used To](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Used To And Be Used To).
