# Common Errors

Recurring Vietnamese→English transfer mistakes — the patterns behind most error-identification questions and writing losses.

## Articles and Nouns

- Missing article: _I am student._ → _I am **a** student._ — see [Articles](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Articles)
- Uncountable made plural: _many informations, advices, furnitures, homeworks, a news_ → information, advice, furniture, homework, a piece of news — see [Nouns](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Nouns)
- Missing plural -s after quantities: _three year ago_ → three years ago.

## Subject-Verb Agreement

- Third-person -s dropped: _She don't like it._ → _She doesn't like it._; _He go_ → _He goes._
- Plural verb after each/every/everyone: _Everyone are here_ → _Everyone **is** here._
- Verb agreeing with the nearest noun instead of the subject: _The quality of products are good._ → **is** good — see [Subject Verb Agreement](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Subject Verb Agreement).

## Tense and Verb Forms

- Present perfect with a finished time: _I have seen him yesterday._ → _I **saw** him yesterday._ (yesterday forces past simple)
- Continuous on a stative verb: _I am knowing the answer._ → _I know..._ — see [Verb Tenses](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Verb Tenses)
- Missing "be" in the passive: _The house built in 1990._ → _**was** built._
- Wrong -ing/to-infinitive: _enjoy to play_ → enjoy **playing**; _suggest to go_ → suggest **going**; _look forward to see_ → look forward to **seeing** — see [Infinitives And Gerunds](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Infinitives And Gerunds).

## Prepositions

- Dropping or adding: _listen music_ → listen **to** music; _discuss about_ → discuss; _comprise of_ → comprise; _enter into the room_ → enter the room; _married with_ → married **to**; _similar with_ → similar **to**; _good in_ → good **at**.
- in/on/at time-place mix-ups — see [Prepositions](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Prepositions).

## Word Order

- Adverb placement: _I very like it._ → _I like it very much._; _She speaks English very good._ → very **well**.
- Inversion in embedded questions: _Do you know where is he?_ → _where he is_ — see [Questions And Question Tags](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Questions And Question Tags).
- Double subject: _My father he works in a bank._ → drop "he".

## Word Choice and Form

- -ed/-ing adjectives: _I am boring._ → _I am **bored**._ (you feel bored; the film is boring) — see [Adjectives And Adverbs](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Adjectives And Adverbs)
- Wrong part of speech: _He is a success man._ → a **successful** man; _the important of_ → the **importance** of.
- Double comparative: _more better_ → **better** — see [Comparisons](/English/Grammar/Concepts/Comparisons).
- "very" + verb: _I very like_ → like ... **very much**.
- learn/teach, borrow/lend confusion — see [Confusable Words](/English/Vocabulary/Concepts/Confusable Words).

## Pronunciation-Driven Spelling

- wich → which, becuase → because, goverment → government, enviroment → environment, freind → friend.

## How to Check for Errors

Scan in this order — the four classic error classes:

1. **Verb:** tense, agreement, form (to/-ing/bare), passive "be".
2. **Noun:** countable/uncountable, plural -s, article.
3. **Word form:** adjective vs adverb vs noun in the gap.
4. **Connector/preposition:** although-but doubled, because + noun vs clause, dependent preposition.

## Notes

- Keeping a personal error log (error → correction → rule) is the fastest way to shrink this list.
- Most errors come from translating Vietnamese word-for-word — think in English patterns, not Vietnamese sentences.
