# IELTS Listening And Reading

## Listening

- **Duration:** ~30 minutes of audio (heard once only) + answer-transfer time.
- **Structure:** 4 parts × 10 questions = **40 questions**.
  - **Part 1:** two-person conversation in an everyday context (booking a room, asking for travel information).
  - **Part 2:** monologue in an everyday context (introducing community facilities).
  - **Part 3:** conversation (up to 4 speakers) in an educational/training context (tutor and students discussing an assignment).
  - **Part 4:** academic monologue (a university lecture).
- **Accents:** varied — British, Australian, New Zealand, North American.
- **Question types:** multiple choice, matching, map/diagram labelling, form/table/flow-chart completion, sentence completion, short answers — all with word limits.
- **Scoring:** 1 point per correct answer → converted to a 1-9 band.

Source: [IELTS Academic Listening format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-listening).

## Reading (Academic)

- **Duration:** 60 minutes.
- **Structure:** 3 passages totalling **2,150-2,750 words**, **40 questions**.
- **Sources:** books, journals, magazines, newspapers — no specialist knowledge required.
- **Question types (11):** multiple choice; True/False/Not Given; Yes/No/Not Given; matching information; matching headings; matching features; matching sentence endings; sentence completion; summary/note/table/flow-chart completion; diagram label completion; short-answer questions.
- **Scoring:** 1 point per correct answer → converted to a 1-9 band.

Source: [IELTS Academic Reading format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-reading).

**Note (General Training):** the same 3-section structure but with everyday/workplace texts (ads, internal notices, user manuals) instead of academic prose, and a lower overall difficulty. Source: [IELTS General Training test format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-general-training-test).

## Test-Day Tips (General Principles)

- **Read the questions before listening/reading the passage** to identify the keywords to hunt for.
- **Paraphrase is the core skill:** the paper almost always restates the text (synonyms, restructured sentences) — train synonym recognition instead of searching for exact words.
- **Manage Reading time:** ~20 minutes per passage including answer transfer; don't get stuck on one hard question.
- **Never leave blanks** — there is no penalty for wrong answers, so always guess.
- **Respect word limits** (e.g. "NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS") — exceeding them scores as wrong even if the meaning is right.
