## HASH

- **Repository**: https://github.com/hashintel/hash
- **Description**: Open-source, multi-tenant, self-hostable knowledge graph platform for organizing and managing information
- **Features**:
  - Knowledge graph visualization
  - Multi-tenant architecture
  - Self-hostable
  - Collaborative knowledge management
  - Relationship mapping
- **Use Cases**: Knowledge organization, information architecture, collaborative knowledge bases
