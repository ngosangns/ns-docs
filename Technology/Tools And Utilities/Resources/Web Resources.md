# Web Resources

Two websites are collected here, one for live radio and one for converting files. Radio Garden plays live radio from cities around the world on an interactive globe, including stations in Ho Chi Minh City. VERT.sh converts images, audio and documents on the reader's own device and sends video work to remote servers for speed. It sets no file size limits and charges nothing for the conversion.

- [Radio Garden is a website that lets users listen to live radio from all over the world through an interactive 3D map interface. Users can spin the globe to explore thousands of local radio stations in each city, including Ho Chi Minh City, and listen live to broadcasts in good audio quality. It is a great tool for exploring the music culture and news of many different countries visually and for free.](https://radio.garden/visit/ho-chi-minh-city/WGdBGTHr)
- [VERT.sh is an open-source online file converter that handles images, audio, and documents entirely on your device for maximum privacy. Video conversions are processed on fast remote servers. The tool supports a wide range of file formats with no file size limits, no ads, and no cost. It is ideal for users who need quick, private file conversions without installing software, making it a convenient and privacy-focused alternative to other online converters.](https://vert.sh/)

Radio Garden is the listening site in this pair. It plays live radio from cities around the world through an interactive 3D globe. You spin the globe, choose a city, and open a local station mapped there. Each city includes thousands of local stations. Ho Chi Minh City is one of them, and the broadcast is live. Audio quality is described as good, and listening is free. The globe is a visual way to explore music and news from many countries. VERT.sh is the open-source converter beside it, and it runs in the browser. Images, audio, and documents are converted on the reader's own device so the file stays local. Video jobs go to remote servers so they finish faster. Formats cover a wide range. There is no file size limit, there are no ads, and the conversion costs nothing. No software has to be installed. It is a private and quick alternative to other online converters.
