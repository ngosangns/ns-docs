# Computer Vision & Video Processing

## Ev-DeblurVSR

- **Repository**: https://github.com/DachunKai/Ev-DeblurVSR
- **Description**: Event-Enhanced Blurry Video Super-Resolution (AAAI 2025). Official PyTorch implementation for enhancing blurry video resolution using event cameras
- **Features**:
  - 4x video super-resolution
  - Event-enhanced deblurring
  - Supports synthetic (GoPro, BSD) and real-world (NCER) datasets
  - Pre-trained models available
- **Tech Stack**: PyTorch, CUDA, BasicSR
- **Paper**: AAAI 2025
- **License**: Apache-2.0

## SimCC

- **Repository**: https://github.com/leeyegy/SimCC
- **Description**: SimCC (Simple Coordinate Classification) - A simple coordinate classification perspective for human pose estimation (ECCV 2022 Oral). Alternative to traditional 2D heatmap representation
- **Features**:
  - Reduces quantization error by coordinate representation design
  - Eliminates need for extra post-processing
  - Better performance on low-resolution images
  - No need for multiple upsample layers
- **Tech Stack**: PyTorch
- **Paper**: ECCV 2022
- **Use Cases**: Human pose estimation, keypoint detection
