# IT Job Interview Notes

## Self-Introduction

- Full name
- Years of experience
- Tech interests
- Goal (digital nomad)

## Questions to Ask the Company

### Before Speaking with HR

- Who will I be interviewed by?
- How many interview steps are there?
- Who are the interviewers?

### Ask HR

- Is the company 100% Japanese-owned?
- Does the company have testers?
- Is insurance paid on the full salary or on the regional base salary?
- Is the contract fixed-term or indefinite?
- How often are salaries reviewed?
- Is the director MITANI MASASHI often in the office?
- How many team-building events per year? Is attendance mandatory?
- Does the company have a fridge or food available?
- Are there any office rules?
- Does the company wifi block Twitter or YouTube?
- Is there a flag salute or a slogan to recite?
- Are there any binding commitments when signing the contract?

### Ask the Leader

- Does the project involve microservices, caching, query optimization, or a design system?
- What is the company's main tech stack? Does it depend on the client?
- What is the scope of the project? Does it include DevOps or testing?
- Does the leader code with the team or only manage the project?
- Will I work directly with Japanese bosses or Japanese clients? Could that happen in the future?
- Are multiple projects run in parallel over a period of time?
- Are meetings frequent? Does the company follow Scrum Agile or Waterfall?
- Can you give some feedback on my CV?
- What does the promotion path look like?
- Does the job actually need Laravel, PHP, and VueJS?
- What do the workshops usually cover?

## Notes

- Interviewer laughs a lot ⇒ a bad sign
- Japanese companies value people who stay loyal to the company
- Japanese people like those who follow rules and processes
- Japanese companies look for people with potential (little experience or fresh graduates) to train
- Experience from Japanese-company interviews: candidates who say they want to learn more about their specialty after joining fail
