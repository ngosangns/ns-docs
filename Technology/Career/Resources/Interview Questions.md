# Interview Questions

## Common Questions

### Why Did You Apply to This Company?

- Saw many technologies and projects on the website and wanted to learn
- The environment has many young, energetic, and cheerful people

### How Far Has Your Learning Gone?

- HTML, HTML5, CSS, CSS3
- JavaScript: HTTP requests, calling APIs, async-await
- Framework: Life cycle (React)
- **Note**: Be specific, and research and practice more

### What Projects Have You Done, and Which Parts Did You Support?

- Example: Project X at school, supported part Y, including...

### Future Direction? Goals?

- Go deeper into web technology and advance in web programming

### Ready to Work with New Technologies?

- Show your ability to explore and learn

### Questions for the Company?

- Internship salary
- Whether they provide an internship confirmation letter
- Salary review
- Tet bonus

### Are You Still Studying?

- Currently in the final semester of year 5
