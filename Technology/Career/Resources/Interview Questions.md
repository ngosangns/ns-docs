# Interview Questions

These are sample answers for the questions on this page. Apply because the site showed many technologies and projects to learn, and the people seemed young, energetic, and cheerful. Study so far covers HTML and HTML5, CSS and CSS3, JavaScript HTTP requests, API calls, and async-await, plus the React life cycle. Be specific, and research and practice more. For a project, name school project X and the part supported. The goal is to go deeper in web technology and advance in web programming. Show that you can explore and learn new tools. Ask about internship salary, a confirmation letter, salary review, and a Tet bonus. The study answer is the final semester of year 5.

## Common Questions

### Why Did You Apply to This Company?

- Saw many technologies and projects on the website and wanted to learn
- The environment has many young, energetic, and cheerful people

### How Far Has Your Learning Gone?

- HTML, HTML5, CSS, CSS3
- JavaScript: HTTP requests, calling APIs, async-await
- Framework: Life cycle (React)
- **Note**: Be specific, and research and practice more

### What Projects Have You Done, and Which Parts Did You Support?

- Example: Project X at school, supported part Y, including...

### Future Direction? Goals?

- Go deeper into web technology and advance in web programming

### Ready to Work with New Technologies?

- Show your ability to explore and learn

### Questions for the Company?

- Internship salary
- Whether they provide an internship confirmation letter
- Salary review
- Tet bonus

### Are You Still Studying?

- Currently in the final semester of year 5
