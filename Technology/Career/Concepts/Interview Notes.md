## Resources

- Experience interviewing for a Software Engineer role at TikTok Singapore
  - Study roadmap: Canva design
- **Viblo Interview**: interview.viblo.asia
- **What we look for in a resume** (huyenchip.com):
  - List only the tools you currently use and understand deeply
  - Explain why you used each tool in the project
  - Include external links: StackOverflow, PRs, tech articles
  - Show persistence and a can-do mindset
  - Highlight standout projects and avoid generic ones
  - Show impact numbers (money saved, how many times faster)
  - From 2 years of experience onward: shorten Education and move it to the bottom of the CV
