# Concepts

- [ElevenLabs](Technology/AI/Tools/Voice%20Cloning/ElevenLabs.md) - ElevenLabs is a hosted AI audio platform (TTS, Instant and Professional Voice Cloning, dubbing, Scribe STT, voice agents, music) with a public API. Vietnamese is in Eleven v4, v3, and Flash v2.5 but not Multilingual v2; commercial use starts at the Starter plan.
- [V-Clone](Technology/AI/Tools/Voice%20Cloning/V-Clone.md) - V-Clone is a paid, closed-source Vietnamese Windows desktop app sold by Rainbow Music Led (Hải Dương) for voice cloning, film dubbing against SRT/ASS, STT, and offline translation in 12 languages including Vietnamese; the model and licensing details are not published.
- [Voice Cloning Comparison](Technology/AI/Tools/Voice%20Cloning/Voice%20Cloning%20Comparison.md) - ElevenLabs vs VoiceStudio (formerly OmniVoice Studio) vs V-Clone for voice cloning, with Vietnamese support, local vs cloud, sample needs, pricing, licensing, API, and picks for Vietnamese cloning, self-hosting, and commercial use.
- [VoiceStudio](Technology/AI/Tools/Voice%20Cloning/VoiceStudio.md) - VoiceStudio (formerly OmniVoice Studio, debpalash/VoiceStudio, AGPL-3.0) is a local desktop app for zero-shot voice cloning, voice design, dubbing, dictation, and transcription, defaulting to the k2-fsa/OmniVoice model (600+ languages, Vietnamese included). The app code is AGPL; model weight terms are separate.
