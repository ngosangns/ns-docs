- https://github.com/meangrinch/MangaTranslator — Gradio web app, YOLO+SAM segmentation, Flux.2/Kontext inpainting, 59 languages. 175 stars. Apache-2.0

- https://github.com/AntonRls/Manga-Translator — C# WPF desktop, manual selection + DeepL/Google/OpenAI. 29 stars
