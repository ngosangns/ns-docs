# Concepts

- [3D Models](Technology/AI/Tools/GenAI/3D%20Models.md) - Tools and models for AI 3D asset generation, text-to-motion animation, and code-driven parametric CAD.
- [Content And Multimedia Tools](Technology/AI/Tools/GenAI/Content%20And%20Multimedia%20Tools.md) - Curated list of AI tools for TTS/ASR, OCR, video and audio download and dubbing, design, and other multimedia content creation and processing.
- [Image Generation](Technology/AI/Tools/GenAI/Image%20Generation.md) - Curated tools, models, and prompt resources for AI image generation and editing, including text-to-image services, Stable Diffusion, and Midjourney.
- [Mash Loop](Technology/AI/Tools/GenAI/Mash%20Loop.md) - Mash Loop (lvt1693/mashloop, MIT) is an endless local jukebox. It uses beat tracking and stem separation to loop each song through seamless jump points and mix a library into one continuous DJ-style set. Weeks old, one author, tested on Windows only.
- [NovelKit](Technology/AI/Tools/GenAI/NovelKit.md) - NovelKit (novelkit.cc) is a Vietnamese long-form fiction studio; the public V2 Lite repo is a local single-operator pipeline under a source-available non-commercial, no-derivatives license, and it is not NovelAI.
- [Video Generation](Technology/AI/Tools/GenAI/Video%20Generation.md) - Curated AI video generation tools (text-to-video, lip sync, portrait animation) plus notes on Meta's V-JEPA 2 world model.
