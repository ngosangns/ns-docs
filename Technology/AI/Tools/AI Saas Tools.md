# AI Saas Tools

This note is a working directory of AI software as a service tools, kept as short entries under two headings. The General section gathers broader platforms, including an AI tools directory, a workspace suite with inbox, hub, and drive, and a Google service that needs a Google sign in. The Document section lists research and PDF helpers, a free subtitle translator, a readability checker, and a document translation platform with its supported formats, layout preservation, tone options, and mobile app. Each entry gives the name, a one line description, and a link, with tags where they help. Use it as a lookup list when you need a tool for a specific job rather than as a tutorial.

## General

- OpenFuture - The World-Largest AI Tools Directory in 2023: [https://openfuture.ai](https://openfuture.ai/) #directory
- **Genspark**: A comprehensive AI workspace platform offering tools such as AI Inbox, Hub, and AI Drive to help manage and deliver AI projects
  - AI Inbox: Manage and organize AI information
  - AI Hub: A hub integrating AI tools and services
  - AI Drive: Store and manage AI data
  - Website: https://www.genspark.ai/ #AI-workspace #platform #productivity
- **Jules**: A Google service; requires signing in with a Google account to access - [Website](https://jules.google.com/session) #Google #service
- **PostClaw**: Hosted social scheduler at postclaw.fun, operated by Cassau, LLC. Pricing page on 3 November 2026 listed Free, Connect at $10 a month, Pro at $20, and Business at $50. No account was created. Full note: [PostClaw](/Technology/AI/Tools/PostClaw).

- Hemingway Editor: A tool that rates the readability and clarity of text; Hemingway Editor uses AI to suggest improvements to sentence structure and writing style. [https://hemingwayapp.com](https://hemingwayapp.com/) #writing #editor
- **DocTranslate.io**: AI-based document translation platform
  - Supports 85+ languages and many formats: Word, Excel, PDF, PowerPoint, scanned PDFs, embedded images
  - Preserves the original layout: keeps the document's structure, formatting, and background
  - Customizable translation: choose tone (formal, cheerful, neutral) and subject domain, plus personalized dictionaries
  - Real-time translation: live translation for meetings, conferences, and multilingual online communication
  - Automatic slide and video generation: converts long documents into presentation slides and videos with AI voice
  - Reading and summarization: scans documents and creates mind maps
  - Mobile app: available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad
  - Achievements: Rising Star award from FinancesOnline, joined Google for Startups Accelerator 2024, Top 5 promising startups at TECHFEST 2024
  - Source: https://doctranslate.io/ #document #translation #AI #Vietnamese
