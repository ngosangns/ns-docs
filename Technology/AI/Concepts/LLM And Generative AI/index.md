# Concepts

- [Clef](Technology/AI/Concepts/LLM%20And%20Generative%20AI/Clef.md) - Clef and Clef-flash are Cloudflare's first self-trained decision models, hosted on Workers AI and open-weight under Apache 2.0. They return a probability for every option of typed noul, choice, and score questions, and are Jev-API compatible.
- [Decision 2.0](Technology/AI/Concepts/LLM%20And%20Generative%20AI/Decision%202.0.md) - Six Apache-2.0 decision models from vLLM Semantic Router that answer choice, yes/no, and score questions in one forward pass and return a probability for every option.
- [LLM Overview](Technology/AI/Concepts/LLM%20And%20Generative%20AI/LLM%20Overview.md) - A short introduction to large language models and the three main ways to adapt them: prompt engineering, RAG, and fine-tuning.
