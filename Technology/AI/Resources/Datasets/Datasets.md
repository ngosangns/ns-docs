# Datasets

Datasets collects gym-exercise and media sets for training and evaluating models. The fitness entry is the exercises-dataset repository. It lists 1,324 gym exercises, with GIF animations and 180×180 thumbnails. Labels cover category, body-part, equipment, target, and muscle-group. Step-by-step instructions are in 10 languages. Suggested uses are fitness apps, ML for exercise recognition or recommendation, and health research. The media section collects datasets for video, audio, and images from reputable sources ready for research and real-world pipelines. The video link is facebook/seamless-interaction on Hugging Face. A related note is Vietnamese Language Datasets.

## Fitness Exercise Datasets

- https://github.com/hasaneyldrm/exercises-dataset — 1,324 gym exercises: GIF animations plus 180×180 thumbnails, category/body-part/equipment/target/muscle-group labels, and step-by-step instructions in 10 languages. Use it to build fitness apps, ML for exercise recognition/recommendation, or health research.
