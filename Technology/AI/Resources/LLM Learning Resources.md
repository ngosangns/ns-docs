- [6 Effective Prompt Strategies from OpenAI – Part 1](https://viblo.asia/p/6-chien-luoc-de-prompt-hieu-qua-ma-openai-dua-ra-co-gi-hot-phan-1-E1XVOvkZLMz)
  - Write clear and detailed instructions.
  - Ask the model to adopt a specific persona (e.g., teacher, expert).
  - Use delimiters to separate the parts of a prompt.
  - Specify the concrete steps needed to complete the task.
  - Provide illustrative examples.
  - Specify the desired format or characteristics of the output. #promptEngineering #OpenAI
- [6 Effective Prompt Strategies from OpenAI – Part 2](https://viblo.asia/p/6-chien-luoc-de-prompt-hieu-qua-ma-openai-dua-ra-co-gi-hot-phan-2-obA46OjBJKv)
  - Instruct the model to reason step by step before reaching a conclusion.
  - Use an "inner monologue" to separate the reasoning process from the displayed result.
  - Apply "query chaining" to split a task into separate steps. #promptEngineering #OpenAI
