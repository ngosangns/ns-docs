# TikTok Strategy

How can your TikTok channel reach hundreds of thousands of followers in just 3 months?

Why can every video go viral for some people, while mine are stuck at a few hundred views?

Whenever I get these questions, I answer briefly: "I use AI to help!"

And sure enough, 90% of the reactions are:

• Can AI make TikTok content? Sounds far-fetched?
• I'm not good with technology, it must be hard.
• I tried it, and AI just gives generic, unappealing content.

And... I used to think the same. Until I realized that AI doesn't replace the creator – it speeds up the creative process 10x.

After dozens of experiments, I discovered 3 WORKFLOWS + AI TOOLS that helped me build a TikTok channel faster than anyone. And... today I'll share exactly how I do it.

## The "AI Research Booster" Workflow – Find a Niche and Viral Content in Minutes

Common mistakes:

- Choosing a niche on gut feeling, with no data.
- Making content around personal interests, without understanding the algorithm.
- Spending weeks on research and still not knowing what to do.

How I do it: I use ChatGPT and Google Trends to research trends:

- Find a hot niche: "List the 5 fastest-growing TikTok niches right now."
- Analyze viral content: "Analyze 10 million-view videos in the XYZ niche and point out what they have in common that makes them viral."
- Build a content strategy: "Build a 30-day content plan for my TikTok channel."

AI tools I use:

• ChatGPT: Content analysis, trend research.
• Google Trends: Checking search trends.
• TrendTok (an AI app specialized in TikTok analytics): Tracking trends in real time.

The result? I no longer have to guess. I know exactly what to do from the start.

## Not Bad Content – Nobody Watches Your Video to the End

TikTok doesn't push your video because of a low Retention Rate (the rate at which viewers keep watching).

How I optimize retention with AI:

- Use ChatGPT to optimize scripts: "Rewrite the opening of my video to be more engaging."
- Use Opus Clip to create viral short-form: Convert long videos into short, trend-catching clips.
- Use Pictory AI to add effects and text automatically: Make videos more engaging without manual editing.

Example:

- Before: "Today I'll show you how to make garlic butter bread…" (Boring!)
- Now: "The garlic butter bread mistake that makes you fail on your very first try!"

AI tools I use:

- ChatGPT: Writing more engaging scripts.
- Opus Clip: Creating viral short videos from long content.
- Pictory AI: Adding automatic effects, boosting retention.

The result? My Retention Rate rose from 5% to 40%, and my videos got recommended more.

## Use AI to Create 30 Days of Content in 1 Hour (the "AI Content Factory" Workflow)

The biggest problems for TikTok creators:

• Ideas today, out of ideas tomorrow.
• Post when inspired, neglect the channel when bored.
• Shoot 2-3 videos and then get "stuck".

How I create a 30-day content calendar in just 1 hour:

1. Use ChatGPT to list 30 video ideas related to the topic.
2. Use Synthesia AI to create AI videos if you don't want to appear on camera.
3. Use InVideo AI to edit TikTok videos automatically, adding effects without manual editing.

Examples:

• Health: "7 foods that help you burn fat 3x faster without crazy workouts."
• Investing: "The stock investing mistakes 80% of beginners make."
• Self-development: "Are you falling into 3 habits that are ruining your career?"

AI tools I use:

• ChatGPT: Generating content ideas.
• Synthesia AI: Creating AI videos without showing your face.
• InVideo AI: Quickly editing videos from text alone.

The result? I post videos consistently without fearing I'll "run out of ideas".
